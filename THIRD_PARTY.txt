StemSure third-party and data notices
This software imports NumPy, pandas, SciPy, laspy and lazrs. They are installed separately and retain their own licenses; StemSure's use restrictions do not replace those licenses.
Build tooling setuptools and wheel is installed separately. This source archive does not vendor dependency source code or binaries.
The demonstration LAS is the synthetic two-cylinder example shipped with StemSure 0.5.0; it is not an external forest dataset.
TEMPL, Wytham and LAUT data used in research are not included in this delivery. Their providers' original access and reuse terms continue to apply.
